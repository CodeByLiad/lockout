## Findings
1. Light-mode accent now visibly changes the theme (the original complaint is fixed): page background, card surfaces, nav pill, segmented selection and the filled "Save height" button all shift per accent (lime cream, violet lavender, teal mint, coral peach, steel blue-grey). See `sheet-light-settings.png`, `light-coral-vibrant-settings.png`.
2. Hero card reads as the exact accent swatch in both brightnesses, all 5 accents (`sheet-dark-today.png`, `sheet-light-today.png`). Hero title ("Nothing scheduled") is dark on the fill and readable. The hero fill is identical across styles, as intended.
3. Dark mode primary button: "Save height" is a bright, clearly visible filled button in every state (e.g. `dark-lime-vibrant-settings.png`, `dark-teal-vibrant-settings.png`). The Today primary ("Custom session") is a low-emphasis tonal-container pill on a dark card: text is readable but the pill is subdued (`dark-steel-tonal-today.png`). Not a defect for rest-day, but "Start session" was not on screen to verify.
4. Style picker: "Expressive" label is not truncated, fits fully in its segment at font scale 1.0 (`light-teal-expressive-settings.png`, `dark-lime-expressive-settings.png`).
5. Expressive style shifts the secondary/tertiary hue strongly and arbitrarily relative to the accent: nav pill, Light/Dark and Units selection, and especially the "Save height" button change to an unrelated hue (lime: pink button; violet: green button; teal: orange/brown button; coral: lime-green selection with a blue button; steel: plum selection with a green button). On light teal the button is brown (`light-teal-expressive-settings.png`), on light coral it is blue (`light-coral-expressive-settings.png`). May be intended for Expressive, but the filled primary-action colour no longer matching the chosen accent is worth a human decision.
6. Vibrant style gives very saturated primary buttons, notably dark lime (`dark-lime-vibrant-settings.png`, lime-green #8c0), dark teal (cyan, `dark-teal-vibrant-settings.png`), light coral (red, `light-coral-vibrant-settings.png`) and light violet (strong purple). Legible, but quite loud next to the muted surfaces.
7. The hero's decorative diamond is a fixed olive/brown in light mode and a pale yellow in dark mode regardless of accent (`light-violet-tonal-today.png`, `dark-violet-tonal-today.png`). On light violet and light coral it is a muddy brown on the accent; looks unrelated to the scheme.
8. Category colours / markers: the Today rest-day screen has no category-coloured elements, so criterion 2 is not testable here. The accent swatch row (distinct on every tinted surface) and selected-swatch ring (white in dark, black in light) stay distinct. Needs a day with a session, or the Food/Progress tabs, in pass 2.
9. Nothing visibly broken in layout/text: no overflow, no unreadable text found on the 4 sheets. Environment note (not an app bug): the emulator hit repeated "System UI isn't responding" ANR dialogs mid-run, which corrupted some captures; the affected states were retaken after a cold restart and all 60 images in the final set were checked to show the intended state.
